This technical report presents the Seed2.0 model family and its focus on reliable, capable assistance for complex real-world tasks. During my work at Seed Infra, I contributed to inference acceleration and deployment for the Seed2.0 model family, including model/runtime integration, XG inference-stack development, ACL Graph execution adaptation, continuous integration and regression validation, and precision/performance analysis.
